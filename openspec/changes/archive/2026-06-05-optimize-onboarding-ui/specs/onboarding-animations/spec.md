## ADDED Requirements

### Requirement: Premium onboarding icon animations
Each slide of the onboarding screen SHALL feature a premium, theme-specific, looping micro-animation on its illustration icon when active.
- Page 1 (Welcome): A floating vertical shift animation and a subtle scale breathing.
- Page 2 (Cold Wallet): A continuous slow rotation combined with a gentle scaling.
- Page 3 (Hot Wallet): A flame-like flickering effect combining rapid subtle scale fluctuations and slight rotational wiggles.
- Page 4 (Choose Mode): A protective pulsing effect with an outer glowing ring/border scaling up and fading.

#### Scenario: Icon animation plays when page becomes active
- **WHEN** a slide in the onboarding carousel becomes the active page
- **THEN** its specific premium icon animation SHALL trigger and play continuously
